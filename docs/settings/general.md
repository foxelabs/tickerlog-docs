# General

**Settings → General** controls how the app looks and how it prints dates and times. It has two cards. Every change saves the moment you make it — there is no Save button — and follows you to any browser you sign in on.

## Dates and times

| Setting | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Timezone** | (UTC+00:00) UTC, (UTC+05:30) Asia/Kolkata, (UTC+04:00) Asia/Dubai, (UTC+01:00) Europe/London, (UTC-04:00) America/New_York, (UTC+10:00) Australia/Sydney | UTC |
| **Date format** | May 20, 2024 · 20 May 2024 · 2024-05-20 · 20/05/2024 | May 20, 2024 |
| **Time format** | **12 hour** or **24 hour** | 12 hour |

These change how every timestamp in the app is printed.

::: info Days and months are counted in UTC
Trades are grouped into days, weeks and months in UTC, whichever timezone you display them in. Changing the timezone therefore never reshuffles your calendar or your monthly figures — it only changes how the times read.
:::

## Appearance and behaviour

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Colour theme** | **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your device. | Dark |
| **Confirm before deleting** | Ask first when deleting a trade, journal entry, symbol or strategy. Turn it off and those deletes happen straight away. | On |
| **Show trade count in the sidebar** | Show the number of trades beside **Trades** in the sidebar. | On |

**Confirm before deleting** does not cover everything. Archiving or deleting a whole account always asks, and so does every billing action (cancelling, keeping or changing a plan) — those have consequences well beyond a single row. See [Accounts](/settings/accounts) and [Plans & billing](/settings/billing).
