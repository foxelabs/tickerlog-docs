# Your profile and password

**Account** has three sections: Personal Info, Billing and Security.

## Personal Info

Your name, headline and profile details. Changes save as you make them.

## Security

### Changing your password

Requires your current password, and the new one has to meet the same rules the
signup screen enforces.

### Setting a first password

If you signed up with Google you have no password, so this section offers **Set a
password** instead. Once set, either route signs you in — Google or the password.

### Two-factor authentication

Not available yet. Where you see it mentioned in the app it is disabled and
labelled as such.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of Security. It is permanent and it takes everything: your
accounts, your trades, your journal, your reviews.

You are asked to confirm with **your password** — or, if you signed up with
Google and never set one, by typing your email address.

::: warning
**There is no undo and no export-on-delete**

Export your trades first if you want them. **App → Data** exports everything as
CSV. Once the deletion runs there is nothing to recover from.
:::
After it completes you are signed out and returned to the login screen, with a
confirmation that the account was deleted.

## Signing out

From the sidebar footer, beside Help.

If your session has already expired, signing out still works and still takes you
to the login screen — a session that has already gone is not a reason to refuse
the request to end it.
