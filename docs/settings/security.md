# Security

**Settings → Security** has two cards: your password, and deleting your account.

## Change your password

If you signed up with an email and password, the card is titled **Change password**.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Current password** | Your existing password. |
| **New password** | At least 8 characters, with upper and lower case, a number and a symbol. Must differ from the current one. |
| **Confirm new password** | Must match the new password. |

1. Fill in all three fields.
2. Press **Update password**.
3. You see **Password updated**, or **Couldn't change your password** if something was wrong.

Your current browser stays signed in.

## Set a password on a Google account

If you signed up with Google, you have no password yet, and the card is titled **Set a password** instead: *You signed up with Google. Setting a password lets you sign in either way.*

There is no **Current password** field. Enter a **New password** and **Confirm new password** with the same rules as above, then press **Set password**. You see **Password set**. After that you can sign in with Google or with your email and password.

## Delete your account

The **Delete account** card removes your tickerlog account and everything in it. It cannot be undone: every trade, journal entry, review, account and preference goes with it.

::: warning
Export your data first if you want to keep it — [Data](/settings/data) has a full CSV export.
:::

1. Press **Delete account**.
2. The dialog **Delete your account?** asks you to prove it is you:
   - with a password: enter it under **Your password**;
   - a Google account without a password: type your email address under **Type your email to confirm**.
3. Press **Delete everything**, or **Keep my account** to back out.

If the password or email does not match you see **That password didn't match** or **That email didn't match**. On success you are taken to the sign-in page, which confirms *Your account and everything in it has been deleted.*

Signing in with Google again afterwards does not bring the account back; it would have to be a new sign-up.
