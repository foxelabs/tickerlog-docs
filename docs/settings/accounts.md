# Trading accounts

**Trading → Accounts** is the list of your trading accounts, with the archived
ones grouped separately below.

Adding one is covered in [Add a trading account](/start/add-an-account).

## The details dialog

Clicking a row — or choosing **View** from its menu — opens a read-only dialog
with the fields the table has no column for: starting capital, broker, currency,
markets traded, and the archive date if it has one.

It is read-only on purpose. **Edit** is its own dialog. A single surface that is
sometimes a form and sometimes not is how a "view" gets pressed for a save.

## Markets are derived

The markets shown against an account are the distinct asset classes of the
instruments it has actually traded. They are a fact rather than something you
declared when you created it, and they update as you branch out.

## Archive

**Archive** hides an account and its entire history from every screen and every
figure — the dashboard, the reports, the calendar, the totals.

It is reversible. Archived accounts get their own group on this screen, with
**Restore**.

Use it for a prop firm challenge that ended, a broker you have left, or an
account whose history you do not want distorting your current figures.

::: warning
**Archive always confirms**

Even with *Confirm before deleting* turned off. That setting is about deletes.
Archiving is the reversible verb whose *consequences* surprise people: every
all-time figure you have moves the moment you press it.
:::

## Delete

Permanent, and it cascades — the account's trades and their journal entries go
with it.

If what you want is for an account to stop affecting your figures, you want
**Archive**. Delete is for an account you created by mistake.

## Searching and paging

Same furniture as every other list: a search box, 25 rows per page, and a pager
with a count line.
