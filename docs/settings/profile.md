# Profile

**Settings → Profile** holds your name, your picture and how you describe yourself. It has two cards: your identity on the left, and **Details** beside it.

## Your photo

The identity card shows your picture (or your initials when there is none), your name, your email address, your city and country if set, and the date you joined.

### Upload a photo

1. Press **Change photo**.
2. Choose an image — JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB.
3. On success you see **Photo updated**.

The image is stored as a square. It is re-encoded when you upload it, which also strips any location data a phone camera writes into a photograph. An image over 8000 pixels on a side is refused as too large to process.

If the upload fails you see **Couldn't upload that photo**.

### Remove your photo

**Remove photo** appears only when you have one. It returns you to the initials bubble and shows **Photo removed**.

## Details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Your name as shown across the app. |
| **Email address** | Shown but not editable here. Changing an address un-confirms it, so it is done from the email confirmation screen. |
| **Country** | Pick from the list, or **Not set**. |
| **City** | Free text. |
| **Headline** | A short line about how you trade, e.g. *Options trader · NSE*. |
| **Bio** | A longer description, as free text. |

Press **Save changes** to store them. You see **Profile saved**, or **Couldn't save your profile** if it failed.
