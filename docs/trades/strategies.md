# Strategies

A **strategy** is a named setup you follow. **Trading → Strategies** lists them
with how many trades each one carries.

Tagging trades with a strategy is what turns the reports from "how did I do?"
into "which of these actually works?" — it is the single highest-value field in
the whole product.

## Adding one

Add Strategy takes a name and, optionally, the rules — the conditions you require
before you take it.

Suggestions appear as chips, filling the name and a first-draft rule you can
edit. Nothing is pre-created for a new account: a dozen untraded strategies is a
dozen entries in every picker and a dozen empty rows in every report.

## Writing rules down

Optional, and worth doing. The rules are what the [weekly
review](/review/weekly-review) is against — a losing week where you followed
your rules is a different problem from a losing week where you did not, and you
cannot tell the two apart afterwards unless the rules were written before.

## Renaming keeps history

Rename a strategy and every trade that used it follows. Trades store a reference
to the strategy, not its name, so "Breakout" becoming "Breakout v2" does not
split your history in two or leave the old name haunting your reports.

## Retire, or delete

Same two verbs as [Symbols](/trades/symbols):

- **Retire** takes it out of the pickers, keeps the history.
- **Delete** is **refused** while any trade points at it, with a message naming
  the count.

Retire is almost always what you want for a setup you have moved on from — the
whole point of a journal is that last year's trades still count.

Bulk actions cover **Retire · Reactivate · Delete**.
