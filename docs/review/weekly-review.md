# Weekly review

Review is one entry per week: what the week's figures were, and what you make of
them.

Everything else in TickerLog reports. This is the only screen where you draw a
conclusion, and it is the one that changes anything.

## What a review holds

- The week's figures, filled in for you from the trades themselves.
- **What went well** — and specifically, which of it was your process rather
  than the market.
- **What went badly.**
- **What you intend to change** next week.

## Weeks are UTC

Which week a trade falls in is bucketed in UTC, like the
[calendar's](/review/calendar) days, and does not move with your display
timezone.

## Writing one that is worth reading back

The reviews that turn out to be useful six months later have one thing in
common: they separate **outcome** from **process**.

A profitable week where you broke your own rules is a warning, not a win — and
it reads as a win in every figure on every other screen. That distinction only
exists if you write it down, and it only means something if the rules were
written first, which is what the rules field on each
[strategy](/trades/strategies) is for.

## Empty states

No reviews yet prompts you to write the first. A filter matching nothing offers
to clear itself. Two different situations, two different messages.
