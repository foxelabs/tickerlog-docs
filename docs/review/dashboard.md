# Dashboard

The dashboard answers "how am I doing?" for whichever account the switcher is on,
over whichever period the date picker covers.

## What is on it

- **Headline figures** — net P&L, win rate, profit factor, and trade count.
- **The equity curve** — your balance over the period, starting from the
  account's starting capital.
- **Breakdowns** — performance by market and by strategy.
- **Recent Trades** — your five most recently **closed** trades.

## It is a screen that reads, not one that acts

There are no row menus on the dashboard and no card kebabs. Acting on a trade is
what the [Trades screen](/trades/trades-screen) is for, and *View All* is the
way through to it.

## Recent Trades shows closed trades only

Five of them, most recent first. Open positions are excluded because the card's
job is to show you results, and a row with no result yet is not one.

## Empty periods say so

A chart with no data says **"No closed trades in this period"** rather than
drawing an empty axis. An empty axis reads as a chart that failed to load, and
sends you looking for a bug instead of widening your date range.

## The first run

With **no trades at all** — on any account — the dashboard is replaced by a
three-step checklist: add an account, log a trade, import a statement. The
account step ticks itself once you have one.

The real dashboard over zero rows is a screen of zeros, a 0.0% win rate and four
charts reporting they have nothing to draw. Every one of those is true, and
together they read as broken rather than waiting.

The date picker and filter are hidden with it — there is nothing to narrow — and
the greeting says "Welcome to TickerLog" rather than "Welcome back".

::: info
The test is whether you have trades on **any** account. Someone with a hundred
trades on another account is not on their first run, even when the account they
are looking at is empty.
:::
