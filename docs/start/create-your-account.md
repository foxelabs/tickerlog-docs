# Create your account

You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Google. Either way
you land on the free plan; nothing is charged to create an account.

## Sign up with email

1. Open the sign-up screen (**Sign up** from the sign-in page).
2. Fill in the form.
3. Tick the box to agree to the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**
   (they open in a new tab, so your form is kept).
4. Press **Create account**.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Required. |
| **Email address** | Required. You will need to confirm it. |
| **Password** | Required. A checklist under the field ticks each rule as you type. |
| **Confirm password** | Must match the password. |

The password must have **At least 8 characters**, **One lowercase letter**,
**One uppercase letter**, **One number** and **One special character**. The
button only submits once every rule is ticked.

Messages you may see:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Name is required / Email is required | The field is empty. |
| Enter a valid email address | The address is not shaped like an email. |
| Passwords do not match | The two password fields differ. |
| Please accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. | The agreement box is not ticked. |
| Couldn't create your account | The server refused; the reason is shown on the field it applies to (for example, an address already in use). |

## Sign up with Google

Press **Sign up with Google** and choose a Google account. Google always asks
which account to use, so a shared computer does not quietly sign you in as
someone else. When you come back you are signed in, with an **Account created**
confirmation. Google accounts arrive with their email already confirmed.

If you cancel on Google's screen you simply return to tickerlog, with no
message.

## Confirm your email

An email-and-password account has to confirm its address before the app opens.
Until then you see **Confirm your email**, which names the address the link was
sent to. Open the link in the email, then:

- **I've confirmed it** — checks again and lets you in. Use this if you opened
  the link on another device or in another browser.
- **Resend the email** — sends a fresh link.
- **Wrong address?** — correct a typo in the field and press **Update**. The
  confirmation goes to the new address. A mistyped address is the most common
  reason the email never arrives.

## Sign in

On the sign-in page (**Welcome back**), enter your **Email address** and
**Password** and press **Sign in**, or press **Continue with Google**.

- **Remember me** keeps you signed in on this browser.
- If you were sent to sign in from a particular page, you return to it
  afterwards.

::: warning Google from the sign-in page does not create an account
Pressing **Continue with Google** with an address that has no tickerlog account
shows **No account uses that Google address** and takes you to sign up.
Use **Sign up with Google** there to create one. An existing email-and-password
account with the same Google address is simply signed in — it stays one
account.
:::

Other Google messages:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| That sign-in expired | It took too long or was started in another tab. Press the button again. |
| Google couldn't sign you in | Google refused or could not be reached. Use your email and password. |
| Google sign-in failed | Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment. |

Repeated failed password attempts are temporarily locked out.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email address** and press **Send reset link**.
3. You see *If that address has an account, a reset link is on its way.* The
   message is the same whether or not the address exists, so the form cannot be
   used to find out who has an account.
4. Open the link in the email. On **Choose a new password**, enter your
   **Email address**, a **New password** (same rules as sign-up) and
   **Confirm password**, then press **Change password**.
5. You are returned to sign in with *Password changed. Sign in with the new
   one.*

If you signed up with Google and never set a password, you can set one in
[Settings → Security](/settings/security).

## Suspended accounts

If an account is suspended you see **Your account is suspended**, with a reason
where one was given. There is nothing to do from that screen except sign out;
contact support.
